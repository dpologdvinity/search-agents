NOTES
WHY ACCEPT A WORSE ROUTE?
A search that only ever improves gets stuck: every small change makes things worse, yet a better route exists elsewhere. Annealing occasionally takes a worse step while it is still "hot", so it can climb out.
CROSSINGS
A route that crosses itself is never the shortest: reversing the stretch between the two crossing roads always shortens it. That one move, called 2-opt, is what all three agents use.
