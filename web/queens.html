08 // LOCAL SEARCH · CONFLICT MINIMISATION

N-QUEENS

Place N queens so that none attacks another. A systematic search backs up through the tree; three local searches repair a board one queen at a time. Same board, four agents.
MODE PLAY CONFLICTS — STEPS 0
Click a square to put a queen in its row. A queen in conflict glows red; the empty squares glow by how many queens attack them.
What the hint means
Press HINT. The hint is the min-conflicts move: take a conflicted queen and put it on the column with the fewest attacks.
THE RULES
Each row holds one queen. A queen attacks along its column and both diagonals. Eight queens on eight squares: every row filled and no two attacking.
WHY THE COLOURS
RED QUEEN
Another queen attacks this one. The search minimises the count of such pairs.
HOT SQUARE
An empty square is hot in proportion to the queens that would attack a queen placed there. Min-conflicts moves a queen to the coolest square in its row.

THE LOOP
START GREEDY
Each row takes a random column that nothing attacks. Almost every queen lands with no conflict.
REPAIR
Pick a conflicted queen at random. Lift it and score all N columns of its row in one pass. Put it on a least-attacked column.
WHY IT IS FAST
A repair never adds a conflict, and only a few hundred are left after the greedy start, so the count reaches zero in a few hundred repairs even at a thousand queens.
