        Cart Pole

        Nonogram

        Lost Robot
        
        15 of 16 seeded trials hold within 0.5 m with 2000 particles
        A robot on a known floor plan does not know where it is. Its beams and wheels narrow the belief until the particles collapse onto it.

Labs

    
Playgrounds where you poke at the algorithms: change the inputs, then watch the method respond.

    

      
        Cluster Lab
        
        k-means alternates two steps: assign each point to its nearest centroid, then move each centroid to the mean of its points
        Paint points, then watch k-means, DBSCAN and a Gaussian mixture fitted by EM find the groups. Inertia, silhouette and an elbow chart run live.
        Play
      
      
        Neural Net
        
        curves that no straight line can separate
        Drop pink and cyan points and watch a small multilayer perceptron learn to separate them. Backprop, the loss, and each neuron's own heat map update live in the browser.
        Play
      
      
        Pathfind
        
        7 searches race one map, from breadth-first to bidirectional
        Draw walls and swamps, generate a maze, and watch each frontier spread. Every path is checked against the optimal cost.
        Play
      
      
        MDP Lab
        
        3 ways to solve one slippery grid: two planners and a learner
        Paint walls, rewards and pits, then watch value iteration spread heat from the goal, policy iteration settle and flip, and a Q-learning agent walk the grid to the same policy.
        Play
      
      
        Optimizer Race
        
        3 optimizers, one curved valley, one start point
        Gradient descent, momentum and Adam race from one point down Rosenbrock's banana-shaped valley, with their trails drawn over the loss heat map. The page races six optimizers and lets you paint your own landscape.
        Play
      
      
        Tree Lab
        
        Gini impurity picks each cut; a forest of bootstrap trees averages the votes
        Paint the plane and watch a CART decision tree carve it split by split, one laser per cut. Then a random forest of bootstrap trees averages into a smooth boundary, scored on its out-of-bag rows.
        Play
      
      
        Markov text
        
        Four public-domain texts, counted once, then sampled one token at a time
        A chain that only remembers the last few words. Watch the next-token odds, the context window, and how much of the output is copied verbatim.
        Play
      
      
        Regression
        
        min Σ r² the squares' total area is the loss
        Drag points and watch the best-fit curve snap to them. Squared errors are squares; gradient descent creeps toward the same answer, and too high a degree overfits. Logistic regression draws the boundary too.
        Play
      
      
        Walkers
        
        15.3 m in 10 seconds for the best flat walker, bred from random soft bodies
        Point masses, springs and pulsing muscles. Nobody codes a gait: a genetic algorithm keeps the creatures that travel farthest. Crawlers and inchworms appear; no hopper has yet.
        Play
