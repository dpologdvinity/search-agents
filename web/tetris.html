WHAT THE AGENT IS THINKING
Thinking...
Each bar is one feature's weighted contribution to the chosen placement. Green pushes the score up, pink pushes it down. Every candidate is a ghost, coloured from cold blue (worst) to hot yellow and pink (best).
FEATURE WEIGHTS
Drag a weight and the agent re-scores every candidate at once. The score is the sum of weight times feature, and the agent takes the highest.

THE SEARCH
TRY EVERYTHING
A piece has four rotations and up to ten columns, about 34 landing spots on an empty board. Each one drops straight down and locks.
SCORE THE RESULT
Nine features are read off the board after the lock: landing height, eroded cells, row and column transitions, holes, wells, aggregate height, bumpiness, and lines.
LOOK ONE PIECE AHEAD
With lookahead on, the best few placements are each followed by the best placement of the preview piece, and the two scores are added.
ROWS AS BITMASKS
Each row is one integer with a bit per column, so a row is checked, cleared, and scored with a few bit operations.
