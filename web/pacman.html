      HOW IT DECIDES
      A* ROUTES
      Each ghost picks a target (Pac-Man, a cell ahead of him, or its corner) and plans a shortest route to it with A*. The glowing lines are those routes.
      Q-LEARNING
      Each move is scored as a weighted sum of hand-built features (distance to pellets, ghosts one step away, eating). Weights move after every turn toward the reward actually seen.
      SELF-PLAY
      The weights were learned by playing thousands of games against the same ghosts, with occasional random moves to explore. The rules run on the server, so every game replays exactly from its seed.
