HOW IT THINKS
SINGLE-CELL RULES
A 1 with one covered neighbour makes that neighbour a mine. A number its known mines already satisfy makes every other covered neighbour safe. Each new fact can trigger more.
COMPONENTS
Numbers that share a covered cell form one component. Components are independent once the total is fixed, so the agent counts each on its own and enumerates every layout that fits.
THE MINE COUNT
The mines left over go among the covered cells that touch no number, in C(n, k) ways. A layout counts only if its total matches the board, which can prove cells the numbers alone cannot.
EXACT ODDS
A cell's odds are the layouts where it is a mine, over all layouts. When nothing is certain, the agent guesses the lowest, breaking ties toward cells whose zero would open the most board.
