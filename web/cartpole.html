HOW THEY LEARN
THE PHYSICS
Each step the cart feels a fixed push of 10 N. The pole's angular acceleration comes from gravity and from the cart's acceleration, and the two are integrated with Euler steps of 0.02 s. A fall is 12 degrees or |x| > 2.4 m. The cap is 500 steps.
PD CONTROLLER
No learning. Push toward the way the pole leans and its rate of fall, with small terms for the cart. Gains were set by hand.
REINFORCE + BASELINE
A policy gradient. Each action that came before a long balance gets more probability, scaled by how far its return beat the batch average. The gradient is written out by hand.
ACTOR-CRITIC
A second network predicts the return V(s). The advantage is the surprise: reward plus the next value minus the current value. The policy learns from surprise rather than from full returns.
CROSS-ENTROPY SEARCH
No gradients. Sample five-number linear policies, keep the best quarter, and refit the sampling distribution to them.
